Calculation method & using your plan file

Each asset grows monthly at the monthly equivalent of its effective annual return. SIPs and extra investments enter at month-end and begin earning returns the following month. A SIP override replaces that month's regular contribution; it does not change other months or your annual step-up schedule.

Monthly order: investment returns, SIP and extra deposits, loan interest and scheduled EMIs, extra loan payments, then one-time expenses. Income-funded payments are assumed affordable from salary/bonus outside this model; enter your SIP after those commitments. Investment-funded payments and expenses use withdrawable assets, with the SIP account first and other accounts in listed order. Retained assets count toward net wealth but cannot fund payments.

Loans use fixed interest at annual rate ÷ 12 and a calculated level EMI. Extra payments keep the EMI unchanged and shorten repayment. The final payment is capped at debt due. A requested prepayment above the debt is unused and is not automatically invested elsewhere. Missed investment-funded payments remain in debt and continue accruing interest. Expense shortfalls are reported without creating new debt. No lender fees, rate changes, penalties, taxes or transaction costs are modelled.

Exported JSON contains your starting inputs, asset and loan details, monthly SIP overrides and dated cash flows. Imported files are validated and previewed before use. Calculated results are regenerated. Keep your saved file private. Browser saving is a convenience; downloaded plan files are your backup. If you later update current asset or loan balances, also set the starting month to the date those balances represent. Earlier monthly changes are retained outside the new horizon and excluded; future changes stay on their calendar months. Transaction history preserves recorded investment contributions. The model does not track units or actual market prices.

Combined XIRR measures all investment accounts, including assets kept untouched. Starting holdings are valued immediately before the starting month; SIPs, extra deposits and funded withdrawals are dated at month-end, with final asset value as the last proceeds. Loan balances and income-funded loan payments are excluded. This is projected performance from plan start, not the historical return on your original purchases. It uses the actual-day / 365 XIRR convention. Unavailable or multiple solutions show no single rate.

Absolute return includes investment gains on starting holdings and new contributions, plus money withdrawn for expenses or loans. Inflation-adjusted net worth is ending net worth at plan-start prices. Inflation-adjusted gains discount each contribution, withdrawal and final asset value to plan-start prices; the percentage uses inflation-adjusted invested capital.

All results use fixed assumptions and are planning illustrations. A positive net wealth can include assets you cannot spend.
