Calculation method & using your plan file

Each asset grows monthly at the monthly equivalent of its effective annual return. SIPs and extra investments enter at month-end and begin earning returns the following month. A SIP override replaces that month's regular contribution; it does not change other months or your annual step-up schedule.

Monthly order: investment returns, SIP and extra deposits, loan interest and scheduled EMIs, extra loan payments, then one-time expenses. Income-funded payments are assumed affordable from salary/bonus outside this model; enter your SIP after those commitments. Investment-funded payments and expenses use withdrawable assets, with the SIP account first and other accounts in listed order. Retained assets count toward net wealth but cannot fund payments.

Loans use fixed interest at annual rate ÷ 12 and a calculated level EMI. Extra payments keep the EMI unchanged and shorten repayment. The final payment is capped at debt due. A requested prepayment above the debt is unused and is not automatically invested elsewhere. Missed investment-funded payments remain in debt and continue accruing interest. Expense shortfalls are reported without creating new debt. No lender fees, rate changes, penalties, taxes or transaction costs are modelled.

Exported JSON contains your starting inputs, asset and loan details, monthly SIP overrides and dated cash flows. Imported files are validated and previewed before use. Calculated results are regenerated. Keep your saved file private. Browser saving is a convenience; downloaded plan files are your backup. If you later update current asset or loan balances, also set the starting month to the date those balances represent. Earlier monthly changes are retained outside the new horizon and excluded; future changes stay on their calendar months. The model does not track actual market prices or investment transactions.

All results use fixed assumptions and are planning illustrations. A positive net wealth can include assets you cannot spend.
