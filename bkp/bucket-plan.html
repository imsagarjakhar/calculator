Returns, refill rules & a market-fall scenario

Illustrative assumptions you can change. Enter returns after expected taxes and costs.

Returns are credited at the end of every 12 plan months, measured from your withdrawal start month. Each bucket uses its average of the 12 monthly balances after withdrawals and transfers. Uncredited returns cannot be spent and do not compound within the year. Negative returns are limited to the available bucket balance at year end.

Review intervals run from your withdrawal start month. For example, every 3 years means the first scheduled refill is at the start of plan year 4, after 36 months. This fixed schedule does not check market performance. Refills can sell equity even after a market fall. Bucket 2 is reserved for emergencies and does not cover regular living costs.

Understand the model before using the result

Bucket 1 is labelled hybrid funds, Bucket 2 bonds / hybrid funds, and Bucket 3 equity, following this prototype’s chosen strategy. These are model labels, not product recommendations. Hybrid, bond and equity funds can lose value. Emergency money also needs suitable liquidity.

The month explorer shows projected balances at individual steps within the chosen calendar month. Opening balances are before that month’s transactions. Once the living withdrawal has been processed, the rolling five-year window advances to the following month. The ledger separates transfers from external withdrawals and investment growth. Playback pauses when you edit inputs, choose another date or leave the tab.

At the start, savings fill Bucket 1 with 60 months of inflation-adjusted spending, then Bucket 2 with your emergency amount. Any remainder goes into Bucket 3. If the savings cannot fund both targets, the allocation shortfall is shown. No extra capital is invented.

Each month: apply any selected equity fall first; perform a scheduled refill; pay emergency expenses and regular living costs; replenish a used emergency reserve where requested; then accrue returns on the remaining balances, crediting them only at the end of every 12 plan months. A refill always draws from Bucket 3. Living costs take priority in a scheduled review. If a bucket cannot meet a withdrawal, an immediate transfer from Bucket 3 is attempted. Bucket 2 never automatically funds regular living costs.

A scheduled review tops Bucket 1 up to the following 60 months’ undiscounted spending and Bucket 2 up to its inflation-adjusted target. Monthly spending and the emergency target rise once per year. Emergency expenses entered at today’s prices inflate to the start of their event month. Returns are credited at the end of every 12 plan months, measured from your withdrawal start month. Each bucket uses its average of the 12 monthly balances after withdrawals and transfers. Uncredited returns cannot be spent and do not compound within the year. Negative returns are limited to the available bucket balance at year end. Transfers cannot exceed Bucket 3’s available balance and balances cannot become negative.

There is no salary, new SIP, tax calculation, transaction cost, insurance payout or automatic borrowing. A positive balance at the end only covers the selected horizon under these assumptions. Constant returns and a single optional market fall do not model all market paths or establish that retirement is safe.
