How INTIMETEC calculates your projections

Results use the investment amounts, dates, expected returns and inflation you enter. SIP projections compound assumed returns; loan estimates use principal, interest and repayment terms; retirement models subtract withdrawals and planned costs. Review each calculator's detailed schedule and model notes to understand its timing and assumptions.

These are educational scenarios, not guaranteed returns or personalised investment advice. Taxes, fees and market changes can affect real outcomes. Calculations run in your browser without a database or account. Save a report or JSON plan to keep a portable copy.
