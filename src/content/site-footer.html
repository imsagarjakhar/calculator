How projections are calculated

Each planner uses the amounts, dates and rates you enter. SIP projections compound the assumed return, loan estimates use your repayment terms, and retirement plans deduct withdrawals and planned costs. Each calculator includes its assumptions and schedule.

Taxes, fees and market changes can affect outcomes. Calculations run in your browser. Download a report to keep a copy of your plan.
